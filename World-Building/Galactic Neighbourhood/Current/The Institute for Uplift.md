We don't know where they came from, we don't quite know what they are. The Institute for Uplift has introduced itself in the shape of sentient birds the size of dogs, it has spoken to us through trees which walk, or through Insects, who stand on four of six legs.
From our first encounters, we know that the institute seeks to integrate existing cultures and genomes into its structure. Before that point however, it operates very similar to The Federations envoys. Supportive but disengaged, it nurtures young cultures. At least that is how it has approached The Federation since we declared our union to its representatives.
