One of the few alien species encountered by Human Space exploration, The Pentagonals are a species of Pentagonally symmetrical, mostly flat lifeforms. 

# Physical Characteristics
Their layout follows, as far as we can tell, a somewhat fractal pattern, having a flat core with five two segmented limbs, each of which ends in a flat nub again possessing five, two segmented limbs.
They seem to have 360 degree vision with small blind spots behind the elbow of each limb. They also seem incapable of looking up or down, indicating that their eyes are not articulated.

Their entire body is covered in a mostly rigid exoskeleton, possessing no visible holes except for the mouth at the bottom of their central core. This mouth has been observed to open only during diplomatic dinners infrequently organised only by [[Dr. Terrell]] and one of their species who's name translates to [[Hope for Folk Above]].

We are not entirely aware of what or if they breathe, but it is likely to be contained in the atmospheric mix provided to our human crew, once cooled to 10 degrees centigrade and heavily humidified. They have however also been seen on the exterior of their ships. Whether they were using technology similar to [[Enfield Generator]]s or can withstand the vacuum is not known.
We assume, however, that their native environment is more absorbent of light than ours, as they are known to communicate by the emission of light and have referred to lighting aboard our vessels as "loud".

# Observed Technologies
Their technology is largely organic in nature. Indeed, their ships seem to be a subspecies of their own kind, having a forward facing core and five long limbs which encase an interior. These limbs come apart for the processing of asteroids, defence and, when landing. This exposes the interior structure, which holds a central core, about as long as the first segment of their arms. Upon this core many structures are built from inorganic materials. It also shows organic structures facing in each of it's five directions, both forward, and backward facing at an angle, extruding between the gaps of its arms. The rear of this structure then holds a large opening, believed to be some sort of kinetic drive.
These ships also possess [[Phaser]] emitters on both the core and on the inner segment of each limb. During our first meeting, one of these ships has also deployed a fleet of smaller ships with a similar body plan. Although the interior core had protruded a much shorter distance and could be spliced open in 5 segments.

# Allegiance
The Pentagonals are a member of [[The Institute for Uplift]]. Not much is known about their political goals, except that some of their species wish to pursue a relationship with [[The Fleet]] and some wish to prevent it. Factions mark their ships in luminescent paint of several colours, some not detectable by the human eye.
