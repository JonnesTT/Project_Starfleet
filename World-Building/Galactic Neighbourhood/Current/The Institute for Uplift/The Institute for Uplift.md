We don't know where they came from, we don't quite know what they are. The Institute for Uplift has introduced itself in the shape of sentient birds the size of dogs, it has spoken to us through trees which walk, or through Insects, who stand on four of six legs.

From our first encounters, we know that the institute seeks to integrate existing cultures and genomes into its structure. Before that point however, it operates very similar to The Federations envoys. Supportive but disengaged, it nurtures young cultures. At least that is how it has approached [[The Fleet]] since we declared our union to its representatives.

# Relationship to [[The Fleet]]
[[The Institute for Uplift]] considers [[The Fleet]] as a whole a society under its protection. Some of their Paterspeciae consider [[The Fleet|it]]s contributers a breeding ground, others consider its structure of openness an unrivaled experiment, that would never grow from its own structure. All members of [[The Institute for Uplift|The Institute]] agree that [[The Fleet]] must be allowed to continue as long as its institutional power permits. Then it must be absorbed to allow the potential of all it's contributor states to shape their vassals.

[[The Fleet]] considers [[The Institute for Uplift]] as a threat to its ideology. The worst kind of enemy, its mere existence provides proof that the order of [[The Fleet]] is not the only one that may survive deep time. But [[The Fleet]] is [[The Fleet]] is [[The Fleet]], and thus, [[The Institute for Uplift]] has a right to exist for as long as it's members will perpetuate it. But so does [[The Fleet]], and it's contributers work tirelessly to perpetuate it's ideals even under members of [[The Institute for Uplift|The Institute]]. Thus, formally, [[The Institute for Uplift|The Institute]] allows any of its members to emigrate to the territory of [[The Fleet]] just as it eats at [[The Fleet]]s territory.


# Leading Races of [[The Institute for Uplift|The Institute]]
[[The Institute for Uplift]] is comprised of many sapient races. Most we have met are derived from humans through various genetic enhancement and eugenics programs. But we have also had the pleasure to encounter star faring Dolphins, Bureaucrat Chimps and an infantry force of Elephants and Gorillas. 
It seems the Earthling races are rather young within its order, as their Envoys take much more alien shapes. An ossified tree, it's glowing, flowering head creaking legible words only to communicate with us, was last to lead a mission of peace. In its order a strange clade of nine legged insects, symbiotically and technologically augmented, took the role of computer or perhaps bureaucrat. The amorphous fluid carrying the solid envoy, as we were told was a young species raised to provide them services they can no longer provide for themselves.
