# 1. Individual Rights
Any individual who's personhood is accepted to be true or considered questionable is granted fundamental rights.

Servants of the Federation are servants of these rights before all other, including the institution's of the Federation. They may not infringe upon these rights. They must, to the best of their abilities enforce these rights.

## 1.1. Definitions
### 1.1.1. Right to Authenticity 
Each individual may at any point define their own ongoing existance by their full faculties and without coercion or ignorance.
### 1.1.2. Right to Life
Each individual, regardless of species or origin, has a right to live the full timewise extend to which their biology or mechanics are capable.
### 1.1.3. Right to Health
Each individual has the right to the necessities of health including but not limited to nutrition, sleep, medical care or maintenance, environmental temperature humidity and atmospheric composition.
## 1.2. Exemptions
One must accept that, under the circumstances of current existence these rights cannot be enforced universally by any one person or institution. As such, servants of these rights are exempt form enforcing or protecting these rights under specific conditions.
### 1.2.1. Exemption of Individual Incompatibility 
Should protection of any one these rights be incompatible with the protection of any other, those named later may be waived in service of the protection of those named first. 

Should enforcement of any of these rights be incompatible with the enforcement of any other, those named later may be waived in service of those named first.

Should enforcement of any of these rights be incompatible with the protection of any of these rights, enforcement may be waived in service of protection.

Should the victim of infringement as defined above be with their full faculties and able to communicate clearly, their wishes, as presented without coercion or ignorance, on the valuation of these rights must be heeded.

### 1.2.2. Exemption of Mutual Incompatibility 
Should the enforcement of the rights of one or more individuals infringe on the protection of the rights of one or more other individuals, the enforcement must be waived in service of the protection.

Should the enforcement of the rights of one or more individuals infringe upon the enforcement of a different right of one or more other individuals, the right mentioned first is to be enforced.

Should the protection of the rights of one or more individuals infringe upon the protection of a different right of one or more other individuals, the right mentioned first is to be protected.

Should the protection or enforcement of one of these rights of one or more individuals infringe upon the same, protection or enforcement, of the same fight the protection or enforcement of this right of the smaller group may be waived in service of the protection or enforcement of this right for the larger group.

# 2. Collective Rights 
Interconnected groups of individuals form entities capable of intelligent and self-aware behaviour. As the personhood of such entities is questionable, the Federation sees fit to grant them the same rights granted to individuals.
Exemptions as defined above apply equally to such entities.

The Federation sees fit to clarify the intentions of this decision.
As the first right, the Right to Authenticity, provides power only over the self, a cultural group or institution may not assert power over it's constituents.
As the lifetime and health of institutions and cultural groups is defined by the number and influence of their constituents, such institutions must allow for their constituents to leave them without harming their Rights to Life or Health.
