An area of space colonized early in earths history by [[The Spiders]]. Under duress from something outside charted space [[The Spiders]] lured three highly warlike cultures to their own hubworld, infesting their ships and homeworlds with a remarkable swarm nanite. Once the time had come, nothing remained but memory. Each individual affected stored within The Grey, summoned when needed from dust.

As nanites are considered highly infectious until proven otherwise, The Grey was quarantined by all it's known neighbours. Something as slipped through, I'm sure, but if it has it seems interesting in absorbing us.

Around Star Date 38000, The Grey requested to speak to an envoy.
Around Star Date 38010, it requested to speak to a general.
On Star Date 38011 General Shafigulin publicly declared that The Grey is to be supported with exports of technology and highly trained volunteers.
