An area of space colonized early in earths history by a separatist faction.
The Federation respected their wish for a different way of life, and thus allowed them the isolation they desired. A hub of disparate separatist movements quickly developed. Both Posthumans and Technological Puritans connected in a space Station colloquially known as The City, one at the heart of the territory now known as the Grey.
We don't know what happened, but a highly aggressive nanobot was found on every planet of this cultural collective. It deconstructed every cultural artefact, every piece of technology, every last bit of every biosphere if coordinated superluminally. No-one survived.
The Grey sends out signals to ships near its border. It isn't hostile. Nor does it appear malicious. But it does make contact, and when it does it is people on those screens. Human voices speaking. Some have seen lost loved ones understand their position. Some have heard them explain that they could come back. But a quarantine has been imposed.

Around Star Date 38000, The Grey requested to speak to an envoy.
Around Star Date 38010, it requested to speak to a general.
On Star Date 38011 General Shafigulin publicly declared that The Grey is to be supported with exports of technology and highly trained personnel.
