The duties of a first officer are thrice fold.
For one, they are to take command of the ship if, for any reason, the captain becomes unable to lead and assure a safe return to the nearest base.
For another, they are to check the Captain, if necessary and supported by the crew, taking command in pivotal situations.
For a last, be a functional leader, such that a small team of experts may function independently of the ships structures and command.

As such, Ju commonly leads off-ship missions that are not combat focused.
