Dr. el-Shaer, as ships Envoy chose the difficulty task of engaging with all the cultures and governing bodies his ship encounters. Ideally, in such a way that these cultures deem [[The Fleet]], and by extension humanity as a courteous and helpful people who are nevertheless capable of asserting their needs.

His dream is to serve on a purely exploration focused vessel and to one day make first contact with an entirely alien culture.
