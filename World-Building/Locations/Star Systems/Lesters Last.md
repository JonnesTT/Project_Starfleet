# Background
- founded during an expedition brought forth by a "Julian Lester" who left no visible trace of having set down on any of the system's planets
- originally setting up an isolated colony was a safety margin for the use of construction nanites. Their being specialised to terrestrial conditions would make sure they do not leave the planet, even if they run rampant.
- on Star Date 60090, a habitat on Lesters Last B3 was evacuated due to atmospheric containment failure. It was later found out that a prior export of rare earths to [[Phuix]] had contained Lesters failed nanites, which used resources available there to reactivate and later returned with a shipment of [[H1-Crystal]]. Active nanites were neutralised with an EMP, shorting circuits, though it cannot be certain whether all nanites were destroyed.
