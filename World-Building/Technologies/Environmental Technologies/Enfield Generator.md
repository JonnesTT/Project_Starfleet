Compact field generators that displace environmental atmosphere, replacing it with an oxygen rich gas mixture.

Simple Enfield Generators are built into the Coms Chips of most ships and stations. These however don't generate a field strong enough to withstand most pressurised environments, nor do they provide oxygen for much longer than a couple of hours of mild exertion.
While they replace breathing masks in safe, but low oxygen atmospheres, or suits during a spacewalk, they are unable to protect for hazardous atmospheres.

General Purpose Enfield generators on the other hand, commonly provide enough oxygen for several days of extreme exertion. They also have fields capable withstanding several earth atmospheres of pressure. To preserve power however, they will saturate the produced atmosphere with nitrogen, slowly increasing or decreasing pressure as close to environmental pressure as possible.
It is not recommend to remove these immediately after exiting a hostile atmosphere.
A rule of thumb for acclimation is to wear the Enfield Generator for the same amount of time in the safe atmosphere, as they were worn outside of it. Most have a signal light, indicating pressure acclimation and safe atmosphere, however it is still recommended to consult a medical professional upon return.
