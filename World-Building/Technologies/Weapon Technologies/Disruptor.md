Disruptors produce modulated gravitational waves. In practical terms, they push and pull on the target. This imparts deformation throughout the target area, causing, particularly stiff materials, to accumulate microscopic faults through plastic deformation until the material buckles. Depending on the settings, the materiel may chip away gradually or explode away in bursts. 

# Countermeasures
The most common countermeasure during the 26th century is a projection of artificial mass interfere destructively by projectors on the shield grid of an installation.
A higgs field wave at a matching frequency but with an offset of 180 degrees in the targeted area will also destructively interfere, causing the apparent acceleration to be measured at 0.
Highly flexible materials will deform elastically, preventing some of the cumulative damage. Additionally, geometries with multiple, disjointed resonant frequencies from each angle of attack can provide an additional challenge when adjusting the wavelength. Particularly ductile materials might also be used as physical shielding. Liquids and granulates for example transform much of the imparted kinetic energy into heat.
Of course, causing the effective distance between the weapon and target to be infinitely large, as done by the hemispheres of warp bubbles, also prevents damage.
