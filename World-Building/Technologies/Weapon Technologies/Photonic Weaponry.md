Lasers and Photon warheads produce a high load of EM radiation, similarly to specific [[Phaser]] settings. However, Photon Weaponry is vastly easier to produce since it's range of wavelengths is comparatively much smaller.
Usual effects of Photonic Weaponry is the rapid absorption of heat into the target. In simple terms, the targets surface is melted or vaporized, depending on its makeup and the weapons yield.

These are commonly extremely ineffective in atmospheres and are very unreliable against shielded ships. However, many warheads are classified as photonic, as many different, often extremely primitive mechanisms, primarily produce a wide range of radiation.

# Countermeasures
The most common countermeasure is a mere side-effect of the plasma cloud used to shield from phaser fire. As it's temperature increases, the plasmas expands, pressure rises until some of it overcomes the electromagnetic field holding it in place. This forms a kind of ablative shielding which has to be refilled gradually.
