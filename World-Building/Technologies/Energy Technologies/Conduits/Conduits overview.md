Technology used and distributed by [[The Fleet]] relies on 3 key resources which need to be transported to different facilities around installations.

Matter, transported as hydrogen, Energy, in electron-potential and Anti-Matter, most commonly transported in Positrons but in some cases anti-hydrogen is used instead.

# Hydrogen Conduits
Hydrogen transport is the simplest form of conduit in any installation. It is commonly stored either in crystallized pure form for short term storage or diffused throughout the crystal lattices of h1-crystals.
Conduits for hydrogen are most commonly pressurised pipes. These are sealed with lining of a high-density silica weave to minimise leaking and back pressured by buffer tanks of liquid hydrogen.
h1-crystals, which diffuse hydrogen rather slowly, are then used to feed the buffer tanks.

# Electric Conduits
Electric Potential is handled very similarly to 21st century electricity. With conduits allowing potential flow from high potential through appliances to low potential. 
While most appliances use a gold alloy as conductors, most longer range conduits use vacuum tubes akin to those used in Positronics to pre charge isolated potential sinks.

# Positron Conduits
Positrons, as the antiparticle to electrons, are highly unstable once in contact with regular matter. Thus, Positron Conduits are always vacuum tubes that use electromagnetic fields to stabilise a cluster of Positron beams.

When used to produce electricity, the beam is directed at an electronic potential sink. It annihilates electrons inside that sink to create negative electronic potential. Additionally, the gamma radiation produced by the annihilation is captured by high-yield radiation converters similar in function to solar panels to generate additional electric potential.
To balance the electronic potential, an Electron Conduit is commonly directed at another potential sink in parallel.
