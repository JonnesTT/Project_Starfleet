The X-0 was one of the first functioning exotic matter generators. This designation may be used in reference to many exotic matter generators, even specialised ones, it is however a specific machine which is no longer commonly in use by contributors of [[The Fleet]].

The original X-0 was capable of producing a small range of exotic matter, most significant among them being gravitons and inflatons (being anti-gravitons) used in warp technology. Through some configuration it was also able to produce Exotic Hadrons and unstable Magnetic Monopoles.

Most exotic matter mechanics have since been relegated to historical textbooks as most relevant use cases were able to be accomplished through the field effects of with simpler exotic particles.
