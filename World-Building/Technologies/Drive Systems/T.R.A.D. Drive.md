The **T**emporally **R**eferenced **A**nchor **D**rive is a traditional drive system achieved by temporarily anchoring oneself to local spacetime. It is used in a similar way as Treks "impulse" drives to move at sublight speeds.
Many of the sideffects of the local spacetime anchoring are caused by gravity, following from the fact that spacetime itself is directional towards sources of gravity. 
- To hover in a gravity well, one would still have to accelerate oneself at local G away from the gravity source.
- The differential between gravitational acceleration nearer to an objects center of mass makes black holes and neutron stars quite dangerous. Generally T.R.A.D Drives are set with a certain "slipperiness" to account for that, but at times the forces this differential produces may overwhelm the structural struts of a ship.

On earth, the T.R.A.D Drive was discovered much earlier than both warp and Higgs Split Engine and is expected to appear before those but after hot tritium fusion.
