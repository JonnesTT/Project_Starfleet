A naive attempt at bringing Alcubieres Warp Drive into reality. Accelerators were placed few light-years apart such that space flows towards the target.
Like self contained [[Warp Drive]]s, warp highways are a marvel of engineering. Unlike them, a highway can easily be fed from waste energy such as local solar collector grids on its destinations.

Highways occupy a short span in our history. They utilise the same fundamental technology used in the earliest self-contained [[Warp Drive]]s, yet were largely constructed before, hence without them. In truth many Warp Highway projects were abandoned, once explorers caught up with the construction ships. Several, however remain to effectively extend the Sol system and thus cement the economical power of the inner systems.
