A rare commodity produced only by few cultures, Sub-Plank Wavers are an eclectic computation substrait. It allows for much larger amounts of computation to be done at much lower energies than most other substraits. This ability to increase both the speed and energy efficiency of computation makes them sought after by almost all institutions whose operation is predicated on computation.

When Sub-Plank mechanics were discovered, [[Flop Allotments]] plummeted in value. Nevertheless, those issued by [[The Fleet]] were honoured without question.








# Authors note
This technology is inspired by the standing wave theory of quantum physics. The assumption is that, if particles are standing waves with specific stable wavelengths which occur at specific amplitudes (aka energy densities), that waves must also exist in other amplitudes and wavelengths. It is entirely possible that I have misinterpreted this theory or that it will be proven wrong, but if my interpretation is correct, then there must be some interaction bellow the amplitudes at which stable particles can coalesce. And it is likely that this interaction can be observed via inference. Which would allow for computation substraits bellow the scales of individual particles.
