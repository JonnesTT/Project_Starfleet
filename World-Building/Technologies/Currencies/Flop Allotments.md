Flop, **Fl**ating Point **Op**peration, are a currency commonly used by industrial and scientific institutions. 
These are commonly issued as contracts both by there federation and independent suppliers.

# Background 
As we entered the dynamic navigation of space, navigational computers had to become more powerful yet early ships had to stay light. Large radiators also offered a target for driftsam and computational infrastructure has always been an expensive risk to send. For these reasons courses were often plotted in advance and captains quickly learned ways to find themselves somewhere near earth without computers.
Independent space travel didn't come early, but with it came a need to cheaply calculate courses to capture ore rich astroids and valuable space junk. This was the point at which flops became a commodity with almost universal value. One, which could often be cashed at large population centers regardless of owners. And such, the flop allotment became a currency used by ship captains and industrial facilities.

## Federation Flops 
In the current day, Federation flops are issued purely by the flop, meaning no guarantee on computational speed is made. Additionally, federation flops are accepted at any humanitarian or industrial federation installation. This structure helps softly balance computational load over the total infrastructure, as computation is outsourced into installations and times with low demand by choice of it's consumers.
The universal acceptance of Federation Flops and their wide ranging use cases and stable presence of the offer has given these contracts wide acceptance as currency in and near federation territory.

Additionally, Federation facilities are often in competition with local providers for contracts measured in both flops per second and total flop count. Officially, this is merely the fancy of their operations teams, diplomats however are under orders to support these developments only in so far as local facilities remain economically relevant.

## Independent Flops
As independent suppliers offer flops with guaranteed delivery times, their offer can't easily be used as currency. However that quality does provide them the status as first order optimal suppliers on industrial purposes. 
Many of these installations also keep a large cache of federation flops for outsourcing purposes and their computation is not rarely both valued in Federation Flops and advertised in relation to performance of local federation infrastructure.
