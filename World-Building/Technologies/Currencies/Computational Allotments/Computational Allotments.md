Flop, **Fl**ating Point **Op**perations are a currency commonly used by industrial and scientific institutions. These are commonly issued as contracts guaranteeing a certain amount of average Flop/s over a specific timespan. Most federation infrastructure is however configured beyond necessity, this the federation can provide Flop Allotments without time limit and locational restrictions at the cost of guaranteed timeliness.
