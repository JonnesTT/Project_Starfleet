Hydrogen Allotments are a range of currencies and contracts for supply of Hydrogen, a resource readily utilized by many technologies.

The most widely accepted Hydrogen Allotments are issued by [[The Fleet]]. Smaller allotments, measured in Personnel Rations, are issued without an agreed upon retrieval date or location. These are commonly used as currency by individuals.

Institutions most commonly agree upon exchanges with local representatives at federation outposts.


# Background
Most modern [[Replicator]]s use hydrogen fusion both as energy source and to produce most of the raw atoms that make up the Replicated product. 
That alone should be reason for hydrogen to be valuable, but it is rarely the first.
[[Fusion Generators]], [[H.I.S.R Drive]]s and even [[Fusion Drives]] almost always become the reason for hydrogen distribution networks long before [[Replicator]]s hit the scene.
Once such a network is established, regardless of the motivation of it's institution, hydrogen quickly becomes the primary measure of value for their spacefaring subculture.
Once the value of hyrogen becomes apparent and a distributor has established themselves as credible, contracts for collection take it's place.

## Relevance of [[The Fleet]] 
The primary mission of [[The Fleet]] is to establish and secure the supply of humanitarian resources. As part of this mission, it is the biggest supplier of hydrogen in federation space. And while humanitarian missions are the primary beneficiaries of the fleets infrastructure, it is no secret that the diplomatic core uses this supply as a political tool.
The trade of Hydrogen Allotments between systems keeps [[The Fleet]] critical infrastructure, regardless of the local or interstellar political situation. Removing personnel associated with this trade is almost never considered for removal, even if other Federation installations are targeted. 
The fleets decentralised power structure and economics of interdependent reciprocity, provides further stability, as depots support nearby depots readily in emergencies.

All this makes hydrogen contracts issued by [[The Fleet]] trusted beyond doubt, even at the edges of Federation Space.

# Independent Freighters
Almost 10% of the hydrogen supplied by [[The Fleet]] is transported by Independent Freighters, growing higher near the borders of Federation Space.
It is also suspected that almost 30% of all hydrogen harvested in federation space is then sold through independent freighters.
This is intended to allow for safe life-paths outside federation institutions and without their commitment.
These freighters most commonly issue contracts for hydrogen deliver at specific locations and in specific time windows. Due to these constraints, their hydrogen allotments are less valuable, but they don't rarely make up for that loss by transporting directly to industrial deployment sites.
