[[Captain Olson]] has just taken command of a new ship, the transport vessel Dunedin. The ship came with a mandatory assignment however, to transport a prefabricated medical facility and embassy to a star system currently embroiled in a vicious civil war.

# [[Captain Olson|Captains]] Log, Star Date 60013
We were given time to prepare, but the first mission of the Dunedin was nonnegotiable just like its engineering staff. We are to transport a modular space station with approximately 200 personnel to the [[Pincoya]] system. This project purportedly began with the sole intention of offering medical aid to those affected by an ongoing civil war between the planet-born population and the population of their asteroid belt. Naturally, the project grew once word began to spread. The final design for the station also offers a full embassy headed by a diplomat who brings the ambitious goal to broker not just a ceasefire but also a trade agreement which shall cement a peace.

# [[Captain Olson|Captains]] Log, Star Date 60018
We've picked up the medical station for the [[Pincoya]] system and are en route.

While I am not surprised the Dunedin would be chosen for this mission, I don't quite like the idea of travelling into an active war zone with an untested defence system either. We've taken the combined Warp-Shield core online already, of course, but I can't say with certainty that I know all it's tactical shortcomings. [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Her designer and my Lead Engineer]] was forthcoming with its main weakness, the downtime between travel and shield, but both her and [[Tactical Officer Da-Xia|Tactical]] have become somewhat enraptured with it's potential. I suppose that I must trust the expertise of my specialists.

# [[Captain Olson|Captains]] Log, Star Date 60025
We have arrived in the [[Pincoya|Pincoya System]]. As a precaution, we've exited warp before crossing the systems outer asteroid belt and crossed the war zone with shields up. This will add another day to our travels. That cost seems to be worth it as unmarked vessels opened fire on sight. [[Tactical Officer Da-Xia|Tactical]] spotted kinetic missiles and believes to have observed light [[Disruptor]] fire. Our shields held up well, barely rising above recharge load during the entire engagement. We did not have to fire either, as the situation was able to be disarmed by our [[Ships Envoy Dr. el-Shaer|Ships Envoy]] on short notice. A broadcast code to signal our status as humanitarian aid was enough to dissuade further attacks. I have marked this incidents in [[Pincoya]]s records to prepare future shipments.

Hopefully, setting the medical station up will be less eventful. The night shift has already begun preparations to disconnect the power module.

# [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Lead Engineers]] Log, Star Date 60026
Construction is going swimmingly. We've had to be a little more careful and plan this a bit deliberately, but we remain fully on schedule. The tricky bit is that the station remains inhabited during reconfig. So we have to be in close communication and coordinate each sections dis- and reconnect. To make sure no-one is in the gangways and all. Though each module is set up to be impossible to disconnect unsafely, so I doubt anything will happen either way.

The locals are very helpful so far. A delegation from [[Pincoya]] C offered to increase the effective payload of our [[Photonic Weaponry|photon warheads]]. For now, I've asked for schematics out of courtesy and, naturally, professional curiosity. I know that this ship is not meant to be weaponised, but cargo ships do tend to be best served with self contained munitions. I'll need permission for these upgrades from [[Captain Olson|the Captain]] and [[Ships Envoy Dr. el-Shaer|Ships Envoy]].

# [[Ships Envoy Dr. el-Shaer|Ship Envoys]] Log, Star Date 60027
[[Pincoya]] Cs Envoys have visited again. For the third day in a row. They are much unlike their "Belters" who seem to minimise official contact. Though, truth be told I have heard nothing from them so far.
Historical precedent implies that they don't think much of political allegiance, which would be the correct approach dealing with the major powers of the 20th century. Though, many modern states too, have summarily annihilated their fair share of "terrorists".
In either case, I have attempted to reach out to them. Unfortunately, they seem unwilling to give the location of their flagships and stations away by broadcasting anything. A notion that will no doubt require in-depth work by one of my colleagues before a peace can be reached.

# [[Captain Olson|Captains]] Log, Star Date 60029
I didn't think we'd have to embark on a medevac mission ourselves.
We received an emergency signal at approximately 0930 ship-time from the far north of the system. After some deliberation and coordination with the engineering team currently away, I unilaterally decided to attempt a response. We dropped off the modules in orbit with the station as we left to investigate.
To facilitate a rapid response we redlined our warp drive for less than a second. Upon arrival, we found a station built into an asteroid leaking atmosphere from a structure resembling a dry dock for shuttles. Several impact sites were observed and logged. We did not recognise any combat equipment or weaponry among the debris. 
14 life-signs were detected. Most were in or near the compromised area, attempting to seal it. They accepted our help readily, however, three remained missing. [[First Officer Ju|Cmdr. Ju]] assembled a 3 person security force equipped with [[Enfield Generator]]s to evacuate the remaining persons. Two was found entirely unaware of the ongoing situation. Another was pinned under debris in a collapsed hallway. 
A total of seven casualties were recorded. 3 decompression injuries sustained during the attempt to seal the leak, two blunt impacts by debris and three had sever radiation burns on their skins surface. The latter are being analysed by [[Chief Medical Officer Dr. Tecuatl|Medical]] and [[Chief of Security Tahat|Security]] as part of the investigation.
We have agreed to provide support sealing the leak in exchange for their permission to record the incident and investigate the site. To this purpose two armament engineers and three Crewmen have returned to the asteroid base with [[First Officer Ju|Cmdr. Ju]]. 

Based on currently available data, I must to assume that the site was attacked using at least two radiative warheads of minor yield.

# [[First Officer Ju|First Officers]] Log, Star Date 60030
We wrapped up our survey of the attack site today. This one is a grim story.
[[Crewman Juuls|Crewman Juuls]] called me over to an impact site he had examined. I was already on edge. Space is eerie even without the looming threat of bombardment. Shadows entirely black, light that blinds. The only sound to reach you the coms chip at your chest. I made my way to him, slowly, carefully, gazing at the silent radiation burns. I admit it had taken me a moment, but when [[Crewman Juuls|Juuls]] spoke the obvious I too connected the dots. "These burns," he said "are only a skin deep." The warheads were almost certainly photonic.
[[Crewman Juuls|Juuls]] had uncovered enough for us. This was an internal affair, the local staff would handle it. But he had already drawn more conclusions. He pointed at the deck, where steel borders regolith. The light grey rock had been reduced to small glowing puddles. The dark steel tore and buckled around the impact site, still hot to the touch. The regolith bellow, however, remained firm. He explained that this is only possible if the warhead imaged the site and attuned wavelengths for each material hit. A sophisticated weapon. And one with devastating consequences as a single unbleached spot showed. Thrown like a shadow on the deck was the stretched shape of two legs and a torso. We were unable to recover any remains.

# [[Ships Councellor Dr. Freilich|Councelors Log]], Star Date 60030
I had the sorry task of corroborating [[Crewman Juuls|Juuls]] claims by interview. Not all the victims of the attack seemed stable enough to talk about it yet, but I assessed te risk minimal to nonexistent for 5 of them. 3 of them were willing to answer questions.
To their knowledge the Belter militants modified mining equipment and point defence systems to produce their weaponry. I cross checked their statements with [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Fuaráin]] and the [[Pincoya]] C envoys. It seems what the Belters have been sighted with are [[Disruptor]]-like modifications of the tractor beams used in mining, near relativistic kinetics, and short ranged laser grids reprogrammed from the point defence systems of their own ships. 
Of these, only lasers can create the kind of burns observed here. And as [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Fuaráin]] explains, a laser grid would've created precise, point-like, impacts on moving targets, not the smooth sphere of radiation burns observed at the asteroid station.
That does not seem to hold true for the weapons used by [[Pincoya]] Cs operatives however. They independently developed several boson based weapon systems, one of which [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Fuaráin]] described as a variable delivery photonic payload. A low powered photon torpedo, designed to overwhelm the cooling systems of small ships. Except, the blueprints provided to her by the envoys portray a fusion based replacement core with enough power to evaporate the entire device and a couple of cms of regolith at several meters of range.

# [[Ships Envoy Dr. el-Shaer|Ships Envoys]] Log, Star Date 60031
The station embassy personnel and I have declared an embargo of any support to combatants in the Pincoya civil war. This was the result of discussions sparked by a representative of the Asteroid Miners Union, one faction of [[Pincoya]] Cs belters. The embargo has been formalised on the [[Pincoya]] Medical Station and has been sent via [[Warp Coaster]] to nearby stations; arrival confirmation pending. The Dunedin will also carry a copy for confirmation.

Truth be told, I'm a terrified of what happened today. 
We were notified that a squadron of four small ships harassed the station, without major damage, thanks to [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Fuaráin]] who raised shields against standing orders. Immediately upon our arrival [[Captain Olson]] broadcast a stand-down signal. The response took a moment, which he had interpret as indicative of an attempt at attaining leadership approval. We were hailed, shortly after.
A Captain Krej introduced himself. Part of a militant cell, he explained that he understood the station as active support for [[Pincoya]] C whom he called "The Planetary Aristocracy". 
This threw uncomfortable reminders of our own history into memory, foreshadowing the incoming attack.
Our attempts at explaining that medical support is extended to both sides of the conflict then prompted the explanation that would ultimately necessitate an embargo. To make Captain Krejs words palatable, he considers the military of [[Pincoya]] C more reliant on medical intervention than the militia of the asteroid miners. 
At this point, our tactical officer, [[Tactical Officer Da-Xia|Commander Da-Xia]], announced a small object approaching at high speed.
Travelling at nearly 100 km per second and heading directly for the station, it's impact could've levelled a small continent. Needless to say it would've broken through the stations shield with ease. Captain Krej apologised, but claimed responsibility for the object. It was an asteroid, less than 200 meters in diameter but picked specifically for its rocky makeup. Captain Krej claimed that he would not be able to decelerate it now and advised rapid evacuation.

I should not commend [[Captain Olson]] for the actions that followed, but the speed and conviction he presented inspires genuine awe. Immediately, he signalled to cut contact and called for [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Fuaráin]], still on the station: "Imminent impact. Astroid. Radius 200m. Solid. Speed 100km/s. Can the shield take it?" I have already heard her reaction retold among the ships personnel. She seemed completely stunned for a moment, nonetheless, her advice may have saved our lives.
In keeping with her expertise, the captain positioned the Dunedin south of the asteroids trajectory and gave a ship-wide warning to brace for impact. 
The entire ship shook. For a moment gravity seemed to fail as we were flung several kilometres aside. Slowly, systems came back online. "Shield-coil emergency shutdown," [[Tactical Officer Da-Xia|Da-Xia]] announced, "structural damage to the coil mounts. I am glad to announce: We are alive. And so is the station."
I may have gone into details that I'm not qualified to record, but I would like to commend [[Captain Olson]] and [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Fuaráin]] for their quick thinking under pressure. I don't believe I could've acted so quickly to save these lives.

# [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Lead Engineers]] Log, Star Date 60033
Proud is what I am. Sure, there is damage, but the coil was not designed for straightforward kinetics. Conventional arms are much less directional in their total accumulated potential so the coil was insulated but not especially reinforced structurally. No need for that if it only absorbs vibrations.

From the logs it seems that the coil only stayed inside the ship thanks to the square cube law. The shield field was still strong enough to deflect at this distance, but imparted the force over a much longer time than a straight impact.
If I'm right, and I usually am, I might be able to bind the sensors into the loop. Then, if I project the shield over a larger distance, I should be able to double down on this same effect. Play ball at fractions of light speed. Though, I hope it won't be quite as exciting as this was.
Unfortunately, I'm also responsible for the repairs. It will take no less than two weeks. At least, [[Ships Envoy Dr. el-Shaer|el-Shaer]] allowed me to accept material assistance from the locals.
