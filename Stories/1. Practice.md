[[Captain Olson]] has just taken command of a new ship, the transport vessel Dunedin. The ship came with a mandatory assignment however, to transport a prefabricated medical facility and embassy to a star system currently embroiled in a vicious civil war.

# [[Captain Olson|Captains]] Log, Star Date 60013
We were given some time to prepare, but the first mission of the Dunedin was nonnegotiable, just like its engineering staff. We are to transport a modular space station with approximately 200 personnel to the [[Pincoya]] system. This project purportedly began with the sole intention of offering medical aid to those affected by an ongoing civil war between the planet-born population and the population of their asteroid belt.
Naturally, the project grew once word began to spread. The final design for the station also offers a full embassy and a diplomat bringing the ambitious goal to broker not just a ceasefire but also a trade agreement to cement a peace.

# [[Captain Olson|Captains]] Log, Star Date 60018
We've picked up the medical station for the [[Pincoya]] system and are en route.

While I am not surprised that the Dunedin would be chosen for this transport, I don't quite like the idea of travelling into an active war zone with an untested defence system either. We've taken the combined Warp-Shield core online already, of course, but I can't say with certainty that I know all it's tactical shortcomings. [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Her designer and my Lead Engineer]] was forthcoming with its main weakness, the downtime between travel and shield, but both her and [[Tactical Officer Da-Xia|Tactical]] have become somewhat enraptured with the potential upsides. I suppose that I must trust the expertise of my specialists.

# [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Lead Engineers]] Log, Star Date 60021
The captain has scheduled another couple test runs. I suppose, at this point I'm no longer surprised. Still, I have brought it up to him. Like all of those military types, he just doesn't budge. Credit where credit is due, he did foresee a significant cut in the downtime a few weeks ago, but even then he wasn't polite about it. 

# [[Captain Olson|Captains]] Log, Star Date 60025
We have arrived in the [[Pincoya|Pincoya System]]. As a precaution, we've exited warp before crossing the systems outer asteroid belt and crossed the war zone with shields up. This will add another day to our travels. That cost seems to be worth it as Unmarked vessels opened fire on sight. [[Tactical Officer Da-Xia|Tactical]] spotted kinetic missiles and believes to have found light [[Disruptor]] fire. 
The shields held up well, not rising above recharge load during the entire engagement. We did not have to fire either, as the situation was able to be disarmed by our [[Ships Envoy Dr. el-Shaer|Ships Envoy]] on short notice. A broadcast code to signal our status as humanitarian aid was enough to dissuade further attacks. I have marked this in [[Pincoya]]s records to prepare future shipments.

Hopefully, setting the medical station up will be less eventful. The embassies semi-saucer was disconnected from the ship today and engineering has begun reconfiguring the submodules.

# [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Lead Engineers]] Log, Star Date 60026
Construction is going swimmingly. We've had to be a little careful and plan out a bit more deliberately, but we remain fully on schedule.
The tricky bit is that the station remains inhabited during reconfig. So we have to be in close communication and coordinate each sections dis- and reconnect. To make sure no one is in the gangways. Each module is set up to be impossible to disconnect unsafely, so I doubt anything will happen either way.

The locals are very helpful so far. A delegation from [[Pincoya]] C offered to increase the effective payload of our Photon warheads. For now, I've asked for schematics out of courtesy and, naturally, professional curiosity. I know that this ship is not meant to be weaponised, but cargo ships do tend to be best served with self contained munitions. I'll have to get permission for these upgrades from the Captain and [[Ships Envoy Dr. el-Shaer|Ships Envoy]].

# [[Ships Envoy Dr. el-Shaer|Ship Envoys]] Log, Star Date 60027
[[Pincoya]] Cs Envoys have visited again. For the third day in a row. They are much unlike their "Belters" who seem to express diplomatic decency by minimising official contact. Though, truth be told I have heard nothing from them so far.
Historical precedent implies that they don't think much of political allegiance, which would be the correct approach considering the major powers of the 20th century. Though, many of the modern states too, have summarily annihilated their fair share of "terrorists".
In either case, I have attempted to reach out to them. Unfortunately, they seem unwilling to give the location of their flagships and stations up for the time being. A notion that will no doubt require in-depth work by one of my colleagues before a peace can be reached.

# [[Captain Olson|Captains]] Log, Star Date 60029
I didn't think we'd have to embark on a medevac mission ourselves.
At approximately 0930 localised earth standard time an emergency signal reached us from the far north of the system. After some deliberation and coordination with the engineering team currently on the station, I unilaterally decided to attempt a response. We dropped off the second cargo module in orbit with the station, hoping that they'd be able to continue construction, and went to investigate.
To facilitate our timely arrival we redlined our warp drive for less than a second. Upon arrival, we found an asteroid station leaking atmosphere at a rapid pace. Several impact sites were observed and logged. We did not recognise any combat equipment or weaponry among the debris. 
14 life-signs were detected. Thanks to a lack of shielding 11 were able to be beamed onboard immediately. 
[[First Officer Ju|Cmdr. Ju]] and a 3 person security force entered to evacuate the remaining persons using [[Enfield Generator]]s. One person was found entirely unaware of the ongoing situation, wondering why the air was suddenly so cold. Another was pinned under debris near one of the impact sites. The last was found with severe radiation burns on their skins surface. [[Chief Medical Officer Dr. Tecuatl|Medical]] is looking into the nature of his injuries as part of the investigation.
We have agreed to provide support establishing a preliminary seal on the station in exchange for their permission to record the incident and investigate the site. To this purpose three security officers and two junior engineers have beamed aboard the station with [[First Officer Ju|Cmdr. Ju]]. 

Based on currently available data, I must to assume that the site was attacked using at least two Phaser-Warheads of minor yield.

# [[First Officer Ju|First Officers]] Log, Star Date 60030
We wrapped up our survey of the attack site today. This one is a grim story.
[[Crewman Juuls|Crewman Juuls]] called me over to an impact site he had examined. I was already on edge. Space is eerie even without the looming threat of bombardment. Shadows entirely black, light that blinds. The only sound to reach you, the coms chip at your chest. I made my way to him, slowly, carefully, gazing at the radiation burns. I admit it had taken me a moment, but when [[Crewman Juuls|Juuls]] spoke the obvious I too connected the dots.
"These burns," he said "are only a skin deep." The warheads could not be, as we thought, phaser based, but were photonic. Technology the [[Pincoya]]ns themselves had possessed. 
By the depth of the burns, [[Crewman Juuls|Juuls]] estimated, the warhead must've been attuned to the absorption spectrum of the material. They wanted to minimize refractive damage. But he also found a spot that stayed dark, unbleached. Thrown like a shadow on the deck was the stretched shape of two legs and a torso. We were able to recover no remains.
Juul had uncovered enough for us to leave. This was an internal affair, not for us to meddle in. Besides, whoever fires at civilians is too dangerous to get caught up with. But [[Crewman Juuls|Juuls]] had already found more, and I was willing to listen.
He pointed at the ground, where the plating bordered regolith. Nothing special, at least at first glance. It had melted. I grasped for a droplet. Rounded, unlike the rest of the asteroid. Then he said it: "The drops are small and shallow." Whatever warhead had caused this adjusted the wavelength directionally. Clearly, a sophisticated weapon, made not to destroy, but to kill. Nothing these folks would build themselves.

# [[Ships Councellor Dr. Freilich|Councelors Log]], Star Date 60030
I had the sorry task of corroborating [[Crewman Juuls|Juuls]] claims by interview. Not all the victims of the attack seemed stable enough to talk about it yet. I assessed the risk minimal to nonexistent for 5 of them, 3 were willing to talk.
To their knowledge the Belter militants had to modify mining equipment and point defence systems to produce their weaponry.
I cross checked their statements with [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Fuaráin]] and the [[Pincoya]] C envoys. Seems what the Belters have so far include a Disruptor-like modification of the tractor beams used to mine, near relativistic kinetics, and short ranged laser grids reprogrammed from the point defence systems of their own ships. 
As [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Fuaráin]] explains, the laser grid would've created precise impacts on moving targets with minimal damage to materiel. All their other weapons are far from photonic.
That does not seem to hold true for the weapons used by [[Pincoya]] Cs operatives however. They independently developed several boson based weapon systems, one of which [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Fuaráin]] described as a variable delivery photonic payload. A low powered photon torpedo. Enough radiation to overwhelm the cooling systems of a small ship, but not quite as destructive as we've observed here.

# [[Ships Envoy Dr. el-Shaer|Ships Envoys]] Log, Star Date 60031
In keeping with the wishes of a faction identifying as part of the Asteroid Miners Union have the new embassy and I declared an embargo of support to any combatant involved in the civil war between [[Pincoya]] C and the [[Pincoya]] Asteroid Miners Union.
This embargo has been formalised on the [[Pincoya]] Embassy Station and has been sent via [[Warp Coaster]] to nearby stations; arrival confirmation pending. The Dunedin will also carry a copy for confirmation.

Truth be told, I'm a terrified of what happened here. 
We were notified that a squadron of four small ships harassed the station, without major damage, thanks to [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Fuaráin]] who raised shields against orders.
Immediately upon our arrival [[Captain Olson]] broadcast a stand-down. The response took a moment, which he had interpret as indicative of an attempt at attaining leadership approval. Shortly after, we were hailed.
A Captain Krej introduced himself. Part of a militant cell, he explained that he understood the station as active support for [[Pincoya]] C whom he called "The Planetary Aristocracy".
This threw uncomfortable reminders of our own history into memory, foreshadowing the incoming attack.
Our attempts at explaining that medical support is extended to both sides of the conflict then prompted the explanation that would ultimately necessitate an embargo. To make Captain Krejs words palatable, the military of [[Pincoya]] C is considered more reliant on medical intervention than the militia of the asteroid miners.
At this point, our tactical officer, [[Tactical Officer Da-Xia|Commander Da-Xia]], announced a small object approaching at high speed.
It travelling at nearly 100 km per second and headed directly for the station. The impact could've levelled a small continent. Needless to say it would've broken through the stations shield with ease. Captain Krej apologised, but claimed responsibility for the object. It was an asteroid, less than 200 meters in diameter but picked specifically for its rocky makeup. Captain Krej claimed that he would not be able to decelerate it now and advised rapid evacuation.

I should not commend [[Captain Olson]] for the actions that followed, but the speed and conviction he presented inspires genuine awe. Immediately, he signalled to cut contact and called for [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Fuaráin]], still on the station: "Imminent impact. Astroid. Radius 200m. Solid. Speed 100km/s. Can the shield take it?" I have already heard her reaction retold among the ships personnel. She seemed completely stunned for a moment, Nonetheless, her advice may have saved our lives.
In keeping with her expertise, the captain positioned the Dunedin south of the asteroid trajectory and gave a ship-wide warning to brace for impact. 
The entire ship shook. For a moment gravity seemed to fail as we were flung several kilometres aside. Slowly, systems came back online. "Shield-coil emergency shutdown," [[Tactical Officer Da-Xia|Da-Xia]] announced, "structural damage to the coil mounts. I am glad to announce: We are alive. And so is the station."

I may have gone into details I'm not qualified to record, but I would like to commend [[Captain Olson]] and [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Fuaráin]] for their quick thinking under pressure. I don't believe I could've acted so quickly to save these lives.

# [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Lead Engineers]] Log, Star Date 60033
Proud is what I am. Sure, there is damage, but the coil was not designed for straightforward kinetics. Conventional arms are much less directional in their accumulated potential so the coil is insulated but not especially reinforced structurally. No need for that if it only absorbs vibrations and waveforms.

From the logs it seems that the coil only stayed inside the ship thanks to the square cube law. The shield field was still strong enough to deflect at this distance, but imparted the force over a much longer time than a straight impact.
If I'm right, and I usually am, I might be able to bind the sensors into the loop. Then, if I project the shield over a larger distance, I should be able to double down on this same effect. Play ball at fractions of light speed. Though, I hope it won't be quite as exciting as this was.
Unfortunately, I'm also responsible for the repairs. It will take no less than two weeks. At least I'm allowed to accept material assistance from the locals. That should speed things up a bit.
