# [[Ships Councelor Dr. Freilich|Ships Counselors]] Log, Star Date 60093
I have talked with the captain, he will not budge. Some of his chosen skeleton crew are my patients too, but instead of letting me continue treatment he told me to place them on medical leave. I am glad that this would not jeopardize their careers as it does in other organisations, but depriving a man who is in struggle with himself of his work is not a good solution.
It cannot be helped, and his stubbornness is not bad enough to put him on leave. As a result, I will be joining the rest of the crew and personnel on leave in the Earhart System. At least I'll have something to explore as it's fourth planet has it's own native life. Well, we did introduce a couple of bacteria before the biofilter was established but most of these were successfully removed by the planets restoration team.

# [[Chief Medical Officer Dr. Tecuatl|Chief Medical Officers]] Log, Star Date 60093
I protest my assignment to this mission. I joined the fleet to help prevent deaths, now I am forced to resign my post or shepard fourteen young adults into certain death. 
This in itself is an insult to my sensibilities, my oath, and my duty aboard this ship yet it pales in the light of the crimes against the sanctity of life and spirit that are still being perpetrated by New Washington. My very presence on this mission is material support to these very institutions. But I can use this position of support to undermine them.
As I have warned Captain Olson, I will do all I can to see that the offer of universal asylum by the federation does not remain implicit to these children.

# [[Captain Olson|Captains]] Log, Star Date 60093
We have begun our journey to the colony of New Washington. We are to transport fourteen pilots and their universal combat crafts to the border of the Grey Sector.
As ordered, the bulk of the crew and supplemental staff have been granted shore leaf in the Earhart system leaving us with 23 crew as of now. I have warned them that any possessions left onboard are at risk of the Grey, but the crews regular cabins remain assigned.

Like many of my crew, I would like to record my displeasure for this mission. I am aware that the pilots have nominally volunteered, but considering the social order of New Washington, I find the thought to be of little comfort.
