# [[Chief Medical Officer Dr. Tecuatl|Chief Medical Officers]] Log, Star Date 60093

# [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Lead Engineers Log]], Star Date 60108
We've reached [[New Washington]]. The reception was kept short, luckily. Unluckily, the pilots weren't there nor did they come to inspect their quarters.
They did appear to help us construct the cargo pods though. Quite suddenly too. Shot up from the planet like lightning their ships did. Or I guess mechas? Old term, I know, but it's the best descriptor. These slanky things just hovered there for a moment while their commander got around the ships comms net to message me directly. If I were older, I'd complain about their manners and training... ok, no I am that old. I had to scold him, at least a little. As an ally the ship would've patched him through automatically!
Once that was over, they helped us assemble the pods. Them youths even had the gall to question my engineering! I designed these pods! Of course, they're gonna withstand the weight of their ships.
Jokes aside, I'm glad I got to mention the second reason each dock has it's own tractor beam. They help reduce structural stress in emergencies! Timelike qualities of space-time for the win.

# [[Chief Medical Officer Dr. Tecuatl|Chief Medical Officer]]s Log, Star Date 60111

# [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Lead Engineer]]s Log, Star Date 60111
I caught one of them Pilots spying on the warp coil! Immediately got to talking. He wanted to know how we keep the virtual mass in place and I couldn't help it. I just can't pull myself away from inquisitive minds.
He proposed a couple of optimisation strategies. They weren't bad ideas, but we tried about four thirds of 'em already. He did mention a theory that I've never heard off though. Apparently, warp shells vibrate, and thus move through global spacetime less efficiently.
In return for his questions, I started boring holes into him. Would've been literal if I had the choice. 
They're augmented to high hell these pilots. He didn't tell me a lot of details, trade secrets I suppose, but they strummed his brain up in a sort of structural mesh, same cables are used for the neural interface. Took the entire cranium off to do it too.
Most of the tech is in his ship though. Naturally, I've asked to see it but he declined. A shame. He was friendly about it, but a shame.

# [[First Officer Ju|First Officer]]s Log, Star Date 60113
I met one of the pilots on my way to the bridge. He introduced himself as 387 IUM S03 07 or simply Buck. Stood straighter than a ruler on an architects desk until I clocked what he was thinking. "At Ease." I said expecting him to just walk off. But he just stood there.
I had just begun to wonder when he mentioned that he'd like to learn more about our culture. And since I went to so much trouble conscripting [[Chief of Security Tahat|Tahat]] and [[Tactical Officer Da-Xia|Da-Xia]] to fill in at the rehearsal this evening I thought inviting the pilots to watch was a good idea.
