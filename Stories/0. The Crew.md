# [[Captain Olson|Captains]] Log, Star Date 60017
I have just taken command of the newly christened USS Dunedin. 
My first officer, [[First Officer Ju|Comd. Ju Lang]] on the other hand has served in this role for an extended period of time, once under my command, the reason for that choice she has never divulged. I suppose, now that I have requested her for my ship, I may have an excuse to inquire. 
A former collegue of [[First Officer Ju|Comd. Lang]], the [[Chief of Security Tahat|Comd. Tahat Ult Ahmedu]] has requested to be posted on this ship with her. After deliberating with [[First Officer Ju|Comd. Lang]], I have chosen to heed this request.

Unfortunately an extended engineering team came with the ship, or perhaps fortunately, as my Lead Engineer, [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Leenane Ní Fuaráin]], is also the designer of the ships experimental combined Warp and Shield core. I must admit I would have preferred a conventional, secondary shield emitter array due to the downtime necessary to transition between emitter modes, but [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Fuaráins]] willingness to admit the risks of this concept and, more importantly, eagerness to improve upon it does inspire a tremendous amount of trust.

The young [[Ships Envoy Dr. el-Shaer|Dr. Saaji el-Shaer]] has also joined us by my request as the Ships Envoy. It will be his first posting to lead an onboard Embassy, but the discernment he demonstrated during the [[Old Hope]] membership negotiations will be very useful to the missions I am planning to accept.
Further, [[Tactical Officer Da-Xia|Comd. Yuliana Da-Xia]] requested the posting as Tactical officer as a she wishes to retire from the Border. A request I feel honour bound to fulfill.

I have also requested [[Chief Medical Officer Dr. Tecuatl|Dr. Tlalli Tecuatl]] as my Chief Medical Officer and received a request by [[Ships Councelor Dr. Freilich|Dr. Rudolf Freilich]] to join my crew as the Ships Councillor. Both of are currently stationed at the [[Barnard Institute of Biofabrication]]. Thus, my first mission is the acquisition of medical personnel.

Including their compliments, a total of 112 persons are to be transferred to the Dunedin. Holding 134 regular quarters for regular crew and visitors, I believe to be within our tolerances.
