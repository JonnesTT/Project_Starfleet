# blrb
A newly designed starship leaves the dry docks. Its designers chose to continue serving as its engineers but unfortunately for them the new Captain has as much of a personality as the ship does.

# [[Captain Olson|Captains]] Log, Star Date 60003
I have just taken command of the newly christened USS Dunedin after an extended sabbatical. My first officer, [[First Officer Ju|Comd. Ju Lang]] on the other hand has served in this role for an extended period of time. She has never divulged the reason for that choice. I suppose, now that I have requested her for my ship, I may have an excuse for inquiry.
A former colleague of [[First Officer Ju|Comd. Ju]], the [[Chief of Security Tahat|Comd. Tahat Ult Ahmedu]] has requested to be posted on the ship with her. After deliberating with [[First Officer Ju|Comd. Ju]], I have chosen to heed this request.

Unfortunately, an extended engineering team came with the ship, or perhaps fortunately, as my Lead Engineer, [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Leenane Ní Fuaráin]], is also the designer of the ship's experimental combined Warp and Shield core. I must admit I would have preferred a conventional, shield emitter array due to the downtime necessary to transition between travel and safety. [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Fuaráins]] willingness to admit the risks of this concept and, more importantly, eagerness to improve upon it does inspire a tremendous amount of trust though.

The young [[Ships Envoy Dr. el-Shaer|Dr. Saaji el-Shaer]] has also joined us by my request as the Ships Envoy. It will be his first posting to lead an onboard Embassy, but the discernment he demonstrated during the [[Old Hope]] membership negotiations will be very useful to the missions I am planning to accept.
Further, [[Tactical Officer Da-Xia|Comd. Yuliana Da-Xia]] requested the posting as Tactical officer as she wishes to retire from the Border. A request I feel honour bound to fulfil.

I have also requested [[Chief Medical Officer Dr. Tecuatl|Dr. Tlalli Tecuatl]] as my Chief Medical Officer and received a request by [[Ships Counselor Dr. Freilich|Dr. Rudolf Freilich]] to join my crew as the Ships Councillor. Both of them are currently stationed at the [[Barnard Institute of Biofabrication]]. Thus, my first mission shall be the acquisition of medical personnel.

Including their compliments, a total of 112 persons are to be transferred to the Dunedin. Holding 134 quarters for crew and visitors, I believe we are within tolerances. 

# [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Lead Engineers]] Log, Star Date 60004
The Captain is just as stingy with the new Warp-Shield core as I expected. Well, slightly less so. He complimented my willingness to improve my invention. As if field testing and iterations weren't an integral part of any good design! Still, I appreciate such a stance coming from a captain. Them military boys tend to prefer ready-made scrap over genius.

I had a look through his official record. Never commandeered a prototype, which was not what I requested. But his last crew managed to return from the front with half a ship and the engineers didn't have any complaints. At least they didn't log any. I suspect he'd have been happy to keep that engineering team. Gotta think selfishly though, I'm glad to know that the captain of my baby knows how to work with engineers under bad conditions. Let's hope he also knows how to work with us under good ones.

# [[First Officer Ju|First Officers]] Log, Star Date 60005
I wish we could stop these drills. Shields down, go to warp, drop out of warp, shields up. Compare times. Tell them to be faster. Listen to complaints. Tell them that they will find a way. A couple of hours later we do the same thing. Over and over, for the last two days. 
I've already talked to [[Captain Olson|Olson]], relayed the complaints from engineering. Naturally, he deferred the question of good enough to [[Tactical Officer Da-Xia|tactical]], but he did agree with the complaints, it is annoying. Unfortunately, he also thinks that, and I quote: "Good engineers don't usually do repetitive work. They'll have to practice until they've internalised the procedure."

# [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Lead Engineers]] Log, Star Date 60006
Alright, I have had enough with these drills. Every single ever so junior member of my team can reinitialise the core by heart now. We need new problems. Something to sink our teeth into. Something that'll see us retread the same ground over and over again, of course. But always a problem that will be solved. For good. Or at least for now, usually for now. 
I'm going under [[Captain Olson|The Captains]] nose and developing a program to automate the coils reinitialisation. Every console entry we make can be done by a program, every measurement made by a sensor and every motion can be moved by an actuator. This does not need people.

# [[Captain Olson|Captains]] Log, Star Date 60008
We arrived at the [[Barnard Institute of Biofabrication]] on schedule and have just received our new medical personnel safe and sound, minus a few grey hairs. I'm told [[Chief Medical Officer Dr. Tecuatl|Dr. Tequatl]] had been roped into a rather disturbing surgery last minute. A former [[The Spiders|Spider]], and a heavily augmented one at that, wished to return to their humanity. I don't know much about their machinery, but the good Doctor seemed somewhat shaken despite their extensive experience in limb replacement. [[Ships Counselor Dr. Freilich|Dr. Freilich]] agreed that it would be best for them to rest.
He for his case has arranged to speak with the patient. He doesn't expect to be able to speak to many post-humans for a while and, apparently, the patient agreed to answer a couple of questions on their motivation.

# [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Lead Engineers]] Log, Star Date 600010
Done. The core reinitialises by the press of a button now. Even snuck a couple of bypass routines into the automation, just in case. And it worked first try! Needless to say I'm a tiny bit proud. Unlike [[Captain Olson|the Captain]]. He had expected this, apparently. Well, he did congratulate me. Said that I am acclimating well to the lawlessness of deep space. The story of how I got this posting may shatter his entire view of the Sol system.
That did cut the downtime to just about less than 200 milliseconds. Very few baselines could react to that. I'm sure we'll cut it down further in time. [[Tactical Officer Da-Xia|Tactical]] claimed the timing was "a trade-off we would make either way", so I suspect we've passed the test.
