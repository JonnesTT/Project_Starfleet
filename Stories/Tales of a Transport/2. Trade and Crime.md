# [[Captain Olson|Captain's]] Log, Star Date 60068
We have arrived in the [[Ghlegogh|Ghlegogh System]] where we are to receive a shipment of [[Sub-Plank Wafers]]. We're currently in geosynchronous orbit around the birth planet of their culture. It is an odd case, with an orbital inclination of almost 40 degrees these people could identify the motion of their planets around their sun long before anyone on earth could. Still, their culture took almost no time to ponder space. Why they delayed space travel until it became economical is something I do not understand.
In either case, they are known to be dedicated craftsmen, one of few member worlds to willingly produce [[Sub-Plank Wafers]] at an industrial capacity. In return we have agreed to provide a shipment of loaded [[H1-Crystal]]s.

# [[Ship's Envoy Dr. el-Shaer|Ship's Envoy's]] Log, Star Date 60069 (Nice)
The trade went without a hitch. In keeping with [[Ghlegogh]] custom, both parties inspected the delivery of the other, tested a random sample and then expressed appreciation for the trade. In keeping with [[The Fleet|Fleet]] custom the [[Captain Olson|Captain]] offered additional assistance, which was refused.

I'm frankly not quite sure what to make of the [[Ghlegogh|Ghlegoghians]]. They are very polite and industrious, but every time I have to work with one of them, an odd feeling rises in me. As if their politeness is some sort of deception. Still, their track record has been almost entirely spotless since they learned that offering help without payment is a custom of ours. Well, we never would have thought to define assistance as a custom, since most of the cultures we've met either consider it as a courtesy, or explicitly rebelled against the notion.
I have to assume they feel about us like those cultures. Considering we are not always willing to provide the same material support, they must deem us as shallow in our own way. To them, a trade must simply seem a more honest exchange of gifts.

# [[Positronics Engineer Leitis|Positronics Engineer's]] Log, Stardate 60070
My shore leave at [[Magellan 17|Magellan]]'s Port lies ahead and since I got the booth for that weekend, I'll be out there finally getting my custom, self contained Holo-projectors to people who might have use for 'em.
To be quite honest though, I am a bit scared. I've spent months shrinking the projectors down alone. And that's nothing on the time I've spent setting up the routines and decision trees! Some of them can literally perform the simpler parts of professions! Why hire a bouncer if you could just summon a 2 meter wide barn of a man? Okay, they aren't programmed to handle every situation, but at least your one security officer can wield the bodies of three!
Unfortunately, how revolutionary these are is unlikely to sell them in itself. That takes its own kind of work, and I'm not sure I did enough. I'm in the port manifest, so I'd assume I'll get a couple of off-station visitors. I do have my display pieces prepared, but this is my first time actually selling. I frankly don't know how this works? 
At least I have my conversion rates prepared, all calculated down to [[Hydrogen Allotments]]. Of course, I'm open to bartering, not like I'd know the circumstances of everyone, but I would hope to get a couple of tons of h2 out of it.

# [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Lead Engineer's]] Log, Stardate 60073
We have arrived at [[Magellan 17]]s shipyard and are ready to unload the [[Sub-Plank Wafers]]. Seems they got a couple of emergencies in. We've waited all day for the handover. 
In the meantime we've started ripping out some of the patches related to the [[Pincoya]] "field test". We've been allotted a couple of time slots with the station's industrial replicators to replace the patched structural beams as well as the grav coils on several decks.
It might seem weird to replace structural beams because of a couple anchored welds. But I'd rather not hear the shield coil rattle, if we're forced to "engage in diplomacy" with one of [[The Pentagonals]].

On other notes, I'll be missing [[Positronics Engineer Leitis|my Positronics expert Leitis]] during these repairs. He's technically more qualified to patch the positron conduits onto the new coils, but this is his chance to field test the appeal of his personal holograms. He's been working on the emitters since we started construction, and I may have assisted with a couple of the problems, so I really couldn't deny his request for leave. 
I'll make sure to get some overtime in return.

# [[Positronics Engineer Leitis|Positronics Engineer's]] Log, Star Date 60074
Day one at the booth. A bunch of people have been by. Most ask what these things are for. A couple thought it was cool, but only one bought one.
That one was roughly what I expected too. Introduced himself as Karson, wanted to know a whole bunch of specs. Which fields they project and how detailed or strong they'd be. He liked that they managed to approximate solid surface. The lack of detail didn't seem to bother him though. Talked for a while to find out what he wanted to do with that. I was hoping I could add that to the roster, but he didn't tell. I admit, that bit was a bit suspicious, but it's not like I didn't think about making a "companion model" myself. Bet *that* would sell like cake.

# [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Lead Engineer's]] Log, Star Date 60076
We finally got the handover done. Dropped the cargo module off near the loading docks. 
They said they'd need to shuttle the boxes into a hold. Something about how that new container design doesn't dock right. To be Franklin, that part doesn't quite sit right with me. I designed that container. And I did include a standard dock and hatch. Being the literal standard. The exact same standard that has been limiting cargo tech for 200 years now!
Yea, no, on second thought, I cannot believe that they can't accommodate that dock. I'll have a word with the logistics officer first thing tomorrow, find out what's wrong. In the meantime... I think I'll set up a sensor on continuous query to make sure the container is still there when we go to unpack.

# [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Lead Engineer's]] Log, Star Date 60076 Addendum
I can't. I just can't. 
It's about 2300 and the sensor goes off. I rush to confirm one the coms unit in my room and of course, the container is being pulled off the station by a simple tug. Not even a cargo shuttle, a tug. Like they're waiting with a damn civvi hauler just a dock over. Naturally, I call the [[Captain Olson|Captain]] to the bridge. Got [[Chief of Security Tahat|security]] involved too. Figured she'd know how to deal with that type of situation.
So. We hail the tug. No response. Try to open a channel. They refused the handshake. Which is when [[Chief of Security Tahat|Tahat]] had to stop the [[Captain Olson|Captain]] from straight up broadcasting a warning. Apparently, these guys tend to picnic when security gets involved. Instead, we make sure the tug knows that we know and tractor it directly.
Thing about tugboats is, they're built to, well, tug ships around. So, it slows down but it keeps going. It does open a channel though. Some Andre Karson is on the other side. A little bit panicked, he explains that he's from the Karnaz, an independent hauler. Naturally we go to double-check, holding on to the boat though. The Karnaz is on the station manifest and they are meant to pick up a couple of crates here, not [[Sub-Plank Wafers]] though. Neither is an Andre Karson listed as part of their crew. Now we're calling the Karnaz, pretty sure we have a thief on our hands but we do have to assume it's some sort of misunderstanding.
Here's where I have to respect the effort. By the time we finally rattled their quartermaster out of bed, Karson had reconfigured his tractor beam. In a flash, he maxed out the tension of the beam and pulled apart my damn container. A moment later, he pointed that new tractor setting directly at our emitter. The interference was enough for him to sprint away with more acceleration than his tugs safety limits should permit. So within a second he was obscured by the other ships.
That leaves me here, instructing the night shift to tractor every box into the shuttle bay, individually. I'm gonna have to check each of them for damage too. If I get my hands on that Karson, I'll put his cheeks through the kind of devastation reserved for foolish friends. When it comes to that though, it's not my job. [[Chief of Security Tahat|Tahat]] has notified station authorities and it seems the tug was his own. Is? Considering how fast he was gone, "was" might be a more fitting tense.
In either case, she's gonna handle the security stuff and I'm going back to bed.

# [[Chief of Security Tahat|Chief of Security's]] Log, Star Date 60079
I've been working with station security to trace the tracks of the attempted thief Andre Karson. He left little record of his visit. No arrival with his name or face in the last year and he's certainly not on the visitors manifest. The tugboat he used also didn't appear on any sensor readings since. I'd wager he managed to stash it inside the ship of an accomplice.
With no other means, we've started with the old-school approach. The computer is running over the station's visual records from the last week. If anyone matches the biometrics we got over the coms, it'll notify us. We did consider checking for the tug as well but [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Fuaráin]] identified it as a common model. One with 26 on the manifests of barges in orbit with the station.
Station security has informed me that he's very unlikely to leave their space unseen though. So we came to the conclusion that it would be best for everyone if we keep this covert until we strike. That means no added security presence and no calling on civilians.

# [[Tactical Officer Da-Xia|Tactical Officer's]] Log, Star Date 60080
[[Chief of Security Tahat]] requested my assistance to extract the attempted thief, Andre Karson.
We were able to track his movements around a small civilian freighter just at the edge of the station's space. Station security scanned it and found no more than 3 distinct life-signs on board. 

Extraction teams were assigned to each airlock as well as the shuttle bay. Our mission was easy: dock undetected, cut the targets off from all escape routes as well as each other, then corner them to force a surrender. Unfortunately, the Karson had different plans.
The squads started making their way towards their target location from the airlocks. My team was going to apprehend the captain and secure the bridge. For this purpose, a member of the engineering team accompanied us to lock all controls. The other squads approached the crews quarters and engineering.
My squad waited at the bridge's door until the squad-leads gave their "in-position" calls, then I gave the go ahead for a strike in unison. We moved in, and, in retrospect, we should've started shooting when we recognised Karson. Instead, we gave the standard commands. By the time we had recognised his noncompliance, however, he had tapped a device at his wrist. It must've been some sort of shield generator, as the following [[Phaser|phaser]] fire merely dissipated in the air. Immediately, he threw some sort of pulse charge and took off running. A flash of light covered his escape route, though not well enough to hide it completely. I saw the wall open with a push and followed. Quickly, we passed offices and decommissioned labs, always twisting and turning. Eventually we found ourselves in a large cargo bay, between tight corridors of shelves. There, I lost him, but only for a moment. One he used to unleash the cargo bots. He didn't have time to assign me shelve-space, still, the critters were a hindrance as they scrambled through the corridors. By the time I followed, he fled into a long hallway. A bad choice, since it had nothing to provide cover. It took three bursts for this shield contraption to overheat. On the fourth, something inside the gadget blew. And it blew with force. [[Chief Medical Officer Dr. Tecuatl|Dr. Tecuatl]] later told me that they had to substitute several of the wrist bones. Still, Karson kept running through another shot. Now spouting expletives, he turned again towards an airlock. There he was met by one of my officers.
After [[Chief Medical Officer Dr. Tecuatl|Dr. Tecuatl]] was done with him, we handed Karson over to station authorities. They'll organise transport and likely impound the ship for now. 

[[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Fuaráin]] requested access to the gadget, and since there was no reason to believe its creator was implicated, station security complied. This could make my job a lot safer, if we can make it fail safely.
