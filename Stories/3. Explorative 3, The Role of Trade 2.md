# Captains Log, Star Date 60082
We are inbound to the Phuix system to pick up a shipment of H1-Crystals to deliver to a system colloquially known as Lesters Last.
While the name Phuix doesn't look of English origin, it apparently is. A small expedition during the 22nd century left a single family of former scientists stranded here. They seem to have had a bit of a humorous track as they soon christened the system Fish but chose to respell the name entirely. Their children, who were later picked up by a prospector looking for hydrogen rich gas giants, cited uncommon spellings as favoured jokes.
The unexpected presence of the unwilling colonist's known as the Phuix family seems not to have been an issue for the prospecting initiative. Records indicate that the, then adult, children expressed no will to remain in Phuix while the parents would not have been affected during their lifetime.
Now, the system provides several kinds of H1-Crystals produced entirely in Phuix. Thanks to an odd obsession with optimisation by the prospectors, much of this has been accomplished without the need for fusion.

# Replicator Engineers Log, Stardate 60082
We're about to hit the Phuix system, famous for its advanced production network.
What they provide could be incredibly simple. Hydrogen in bottles and crystals. All, including the packaging could be produced from the hydrogen of Phuix E with a single industrial replicator installation. One they surely have on their core station. But no! This entire system was designed by a genius. Slowly and steadily he built a resource gathering and refinement operation so complex that then entire operation has barely had to use it's own hydrogen supply. No fusion for energy or elements, at all.
It's said that swarms of drones fly transport throughout the system to deliver heavy elements to refinement stations. Meanwhile, some of the extraction sites still use bucket diggers, modified to be mostly autonomous but bucket diggers! That's 20ce machinery!
I have seen some industrial networks, but I'd have loved to speak to the kind of person who chooses a bucket digger in the 23rd century. Replicators were a thing, fusion all the way through Zinc just became commonplace. Really, there's rather little he wouldn't have been able to replicate. Or maybe that's just it? He was simply able to continue building. Once he got heavier elements sorted out, he just continued making it more and more efficient every day.
I've requested shore leave on the station, just briefly to find out everything I can.
