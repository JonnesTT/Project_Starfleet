# Captains Log, Star Date 60082
We are inbound to the Phuix system to pick up a shipment of H1-Crystals to deliver to a system Colloquially called Lesters Last.
While name Phuix doesn't look of English origin, it apparently is. A small expedition during the 22nd century left a single family of former scientists stranded here. They seem to have had a bit of a humour as they christened the system Fish but chose to respell the name entirely. Their children, who were later picked up by a prospector looking for hydrogen rich gas giants, cited uncommon spellings as favoured jokes.
The unexpected presence of the Phuix family seems not to have been an issue for the prospecting initiative. Records indicate that the then adult children expressed no will to remain in Phuix while the parents would not have been affected during their lifetime.
Now, the system provides Several kinds of H1-Crystals produced entirely in Phuix and, thanks to the prospectors want to optimise, in large parts without fusion.

# Replicator Engineers Log, Stardate 60082
We're about to hit the system near of the famous Phuix production network. 
What they provide could be so simple. Hydrogen in bottles and crystals. All, including the packaging could be produced from the hydrogen of Phuix E with an industrial replicator installation. One they surely have on their core station. But no! This entire system was designed by a genius. Slowly and steadily he built a resource gathering and refinement operation so complex that he barely needs to use his hydrogen supply at all. No fusion for energy or elements.
It's said that swarms of drones fly transport throughout the system to deliver heavy elements to refinement stations. Meanwhile, some of the extraction sites still use bucket diggers, modified to be mostly autonomous but bucket diggers. That's straight 20ce machinery!
I've seen some industrial networks, but I'd love to hear the mind of a man who built a bucket digger in the 23rd century. Replicators were a thing, fusion all the way through Zinc just became commonplace. Really, there's very little he wouldn't have been able to replicate. Or maybe that's just it? He was able to continue building. Once he got heavier elements sorted out, he just continued making it better. Maybe he became a little obsessed with the efficiency of his harvest? Or maybe the massive amount of oxygen and carbon left over after heavy element harvest just annoyed him?
I'll request shore leave on the station, just briefly to find out everything I can.
