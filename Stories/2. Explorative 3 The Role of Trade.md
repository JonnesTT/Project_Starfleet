# Captains Log, Star Date 60068
We have arrived in the Ghlegogh system where we will pick up a shipment of Sub-Plank Wafers.
One cargo module has been reconstructed and loaded at a station in geosynchronous orbit around the birth planet of their culture. And a weird case that planet is. With an orbital inclination of almost 40 degrees these people could identify the motion of their planets around their sun long before anyone on earth could. Still, their culture took almost no time to ponder space. Why they delayed space travel until it became economical is something I just cannot understand.
In either case, they are known to be dedicated craftsmen, one of few member worlds to willingly produce Sub-Plank Wafers at an industrial capacity. In return we have agreed to provide a shipment of small fusion power cells.

# Envoys Log, Star Date 60069 (Nice)
The trade went without hitch. In keeping with Ghlegogh custom, both parties inspected the delivery of the other. Tested a random sample and thanked for the trade. 
In keeping with federation traditions the captain offered additional assistance, which was refused, before we made our way towards the drop-off point at the Magellan Shipyard.

I'm frankly not quite sure what to make of the Ghlegoghians. They are very polite and industrial, but every time I have to work with one of them an odd feeling rises in me. As if their politeness is some sort of deception. Still, their track record as a culture has been almost entirely spotless since they learned that offering help without payment is a custom of us. Well, they were the ones to define it as a custom, as they were one of the first cultures we met that did not understand such offers as a courtesy.
I have to assume they feel similar about us either. Considering we offer material support which we are not always willing to provide, they must deem us as oddly shallow. To them, a trade must simply seem more honest than an exchange of gifts.
