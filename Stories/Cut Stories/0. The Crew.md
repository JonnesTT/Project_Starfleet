# [[Captain Olson|Captains]] Log, Star Date 60003
I have just taken command of the newly christened USS Dunedin. 
My first officer, [[First Officer Ju|Comd. Ju Lang]] on the other hand has served in this role for an extended period of time, the reason for that choice she has never divulged. I suppose, now that I have requested her for my ship, I may have an excuse to inquire. 
A former colleague of [[First Officer Ju|Comd. Lang]], the [[Chief of Security Tahat|Comd. Tahat Ult Ahmedu]] has requested to be posted on this ship with her. After deliberating with [[First Officer Ju|Comd. Lang]], I have chosen to heed this request.

Unfortunately an extended engineering team came with the ship, or perhaps fortunately, as my Lead Engineer, [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Leenane Ní Fuaráin]], is also the designer of the ships experimental combined Warp and Shield core. I must admit I would have preferred a conventional, secondary shield emitter array due to the downtime necessary to transition between emitter modes. [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Fuaráins]] willingness to admit the risks of this concept and, more importantly, eagerness to improve upon it does inspire a tremendous amount of trust however.

The young [[Ships Envoy Dr. el-Shaer|Dr. Saaji el-Shaer]] has also joined us by my request as the Ships Envoy. It will be his first posting to lead an onboard Embassy, but the discernment he demonstrated during the [[Old Hope]] membership negotiations will be very useful to the missions I am planning to accept.
Further, [[Tactical Officer Da-Xia|Comd. Yuliana Da-Xia]] requested the posting as Tactical officer as a she wishes to retire from the Border. A request I feel honour bound to fulfil.

I have also requested [[Chief Medical Officer Dr. Tecuatl|Dr. Tlalli Tecuatl]] as my Chief Medical Officer and received a request by [[Ships Councellor Dr. Freilich|Dr. Rudolf Freilich]] to join my crew as the Ships Councillor. Both of them are currently stationed at the [[Barnard Institute of Biofabrication]]. Thus, my first mission is the acquisition of medical personnel.

Including their compliments, a total of 112 persons are to be transferred to the Dunedin. Holding 134 regular quarters for crew and visitors, I believe us to be within tolerances 

# [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Lead Engineers]] Log, Star Date 60004
The Captain is just as stingy with the new Warp-Shield core as I expected. Well, slightly less. He complimented my willingness to improve my invention. As if field testing and iterations weren't an integral part of any good design! Still, I appreciate such a stance coming from a captain. Them boys tend to prefer ready-made scrap over genius.

I had a look through his official record. Never commandeered a prototype, which was not what I requested. But his last crew managed to return from the front with half a ship and the engineers didn't log any complaints, at least publicly. I suspect he'd have been happy to keep that engineering team.
Gotta think selfishly though, I'm glad to know that the captain of my baby knows how to work with engineers even in bad conditions.

# [[First Officer Ju|First Officers]] Log, Star Date 60005
I wish these drills could stop. Shields down, go to warp, drop out of warp, shields up. Compare times. Tell them to be faster. Listen to complaints. Tell them that they will find way. A couple of hours later we do the same thing. Over and over, for the last two days. 
I already talked to [[Captain Olson|Olson]], relayed the complaints from engineering. Naturally, he deferred the question of good enough to [[Tactical Officer Da-Xia|tactical]], but he did agree with the complaints, it's annoying. Unfortunately, he also thinks that "It's only a question of how long it takes engineering to internalise the procedure."

# [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Lead Engineers]] Log, Star Date 60006
Alright, I have had enough with these drills. Repetitive action is not the work for engineers. We need new problems constantly. Something to sink our teeth into. Sure, something that'll see us retreat the same ground over and over again. But always a problem that will be solved. For good. Or at least for now, usually for now.
So, I'm going under [[Captain Olson|The Captains]] nose and developing a program to automate the coils reinitialisation. Every console entry we make can be done by a program, every measurement made by a sensor and every motion can be done by an actuator of some kind.

# [[Captain Olson|Captain]]s Log, Star Date 60008
We arrived at the [[Barnard Institute of Biofabrication]] on schedule and have just received our new medical personnel safe and sound, minus a few grey hairs. I'm told [[Chief Medical Officer Dr. Tecuatl|Dr. Tequatl]] had been roped into a rather disturbing surgery last minute. A former [[The Spiders|Spider]], and a heavily augmented one at that, wished to return to their humanity. I don't know much about their machinery, but the good Doctor seemed somewhat shaken despite their extensive experience in limb replacement. [[Ships Councellor Dr. Freilich|Dr. Freilich]] agreed that it would be best for them to rest.

# [[Lead Engineer Ní Fuaráin|Lead Engineers]] Log, Star Date 600010
Not quite sure how I should react, but [[Captain Olson|The Captain]] had expected me to automate the reinitialisation of the warp core. I guess, he does know how engineers tick. Why he didn't lead with that is likely a mystery to all but his military types. In either case, [[Tactical Officer Da-Xia|tactical]] considers the timing "a trade-off we would make either way". At least her critique goes towards protocol instead of me.
